SLOP2710 · Week 12 · Adaeze and Priya

---

## More people need a kidney than there are kidneys

No process improvement fixes that.

Every organ gets used. There is no efficiency left to extract.

{/* _class: centered */}

## The only question is order.

---

## Every rule you learned has been proposed for it

**Arrival order** — transparent, ungameable, wastes organs

**Most life-years** — maximises the good, deprioritises the old and the sick

**Sickest first** — medical instinct, *least* efficient rule available

**Price** — illegal almost everywhere, and the objection is not efficiency

---

{/* _class: centered */}

## So which one did the US pick?

```notes
None of them. After two decades of public argument it runs a weighted
composite that pleases nobody entirely, gets revised repeatedly, and publishes
its weights. That is not a failure to find the right answer — it is what
choosing looks like when you have to do it in the open.
```

## Over-sixties: 42% of the list

## under arrival order.

## **83%** under most-life-years.

```notes
Because they are the people it never picks, so they stay — and they keep
getting older while they do. The rule does not merely under-serve them. It
fills the unit with them and then declines them faster.
```

---

## And the last column barely moves

35 to 38 per cent leave without an organ under **every** rule.

Ordering is conservative. Week 8's theorem, with the stakes changed.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

The only control in the room

that changes how many people die

is the supply.

```notes
Drag it from three to six: about half to about one in nine, under whichever
rule is selected. Nothing else comes close. If their brief can add capacity,
that dominates every clever rule in the course.
```

---

## What the real system actually does

- **waiting time** — now backdated to the start of dialysis
- **sensitisation** — steep points for the hardest to match
- **paediatric status**, prior living donors
- **longevity matching** — best fifth of organs to best fifth of candidates
- **2021** — regional boundaries replaced by distance circles

```notes
Every one of those is a correction to a failure they have already met in this
course. And the design move worth stealing: longevity matching is capped at a
fraction of the supply, not applied to all of it.
```

## The method, in five words

**observe · model · experiment · cost · decide**

## What you can do now that you could not in February

Watch a line for ninety seconds and **state the rule**.

**Measure it** from outside with a stopwatch.

Put a **number** on what it costs, and name who holds the bill.

Propose a different rule and predict **who wins and who loses**.
