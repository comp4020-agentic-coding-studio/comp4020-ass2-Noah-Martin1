# People

Three of us, from three different directions into the same subject: operations
research, airport ground operations, and emergency medicine.

We do not entirely agree, and the disagreement is on the timetable rather than
hidden. In week 7 you will watch Adaeze argue that a queue's ordering rule is
where the distributive work happens, and Priya argue that the rule is only ever
as good as the ninety-second human judgement feeding it. Neither of us expects
to win. You are not required to pick a side, but by the final brief you will
need to have noticed which one your own design depends on.

**Who to ask.** Tomáš takes the labs in weeks 1–5 and reads your raw counts.
Priya takes weeks 6–12 and marks the simulation reports. Adaeze convenes, gives
most of the lectures, and marks the final design brief.
